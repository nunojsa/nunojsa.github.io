passmgmt-rs – Privacy policy

passmgmt-rs runs entirely on your own computer. It has no server and collects no data.

  • It uses Google sign-in to access your Google Drive. It lists your Drive files to find KeePass databases (.kdbx), and reads and writes only the database you choose.
  • Your Google sign-in token is stored in your computer's keyring. Your data is never sent to the developer or to any third party.
  • The database is decrypted only on your computer; your master password never leaves it.

You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.
